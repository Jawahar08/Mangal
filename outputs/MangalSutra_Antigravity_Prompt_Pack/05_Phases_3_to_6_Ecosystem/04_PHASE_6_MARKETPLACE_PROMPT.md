# PHASE 6 — MANGALSUTRA MARKETPLACE PROMPT

Read the Master Prompt, feature index, repository audit, and prior-phase architecture. Plan or build the marketplace only when selected for this phase and when real commerce providers, vendors, operations, and support are understood. Keep it secondary to matchmaking and relationship care.

## Catalog scope

### Before marriage
Gifts, wellness, Ayurveda, skincare, grooming, meditation, books, couple experiences.

### Wedding
Jewellery, clothing, pooja products, invitations, photography, travel, wedding services.

### After marriage
Couple wellness, home products, relationship books, fitness, meditation, and appropriate wellness/spiritual products.

Handle intimacy and wellbeing products sensitively and within applicable policy. Do not add products or claims that are not supported by actual vendors and applicable rules.

## Product and operations

Design clear category browsing, search/filter, product details, vendor identity, pricing, availability, delivery/booking information, customer support, orders, fulfillment, cancellation, refunds, and privacy expectations. Confirm payment provider and vendor operations before promising checkout.

Never fake a payment, booking, inventory status, refund, or fulfillment. Show success only after a real provider confirms it. If commerce is not ready, provide an explicitly labeled discovery/catalog prototype without an active purchase path.

## Acceptance criteria

Users can distinguish product content from relationship guidance, understand who provides an offering, see accurate terms and availability, and access support. Marketplace pages do not overwhelm core navigation or exploit sensitive relationship data for unrelated advertising. Report real provider connections, operational gaps, and the next safe implementation slice.
