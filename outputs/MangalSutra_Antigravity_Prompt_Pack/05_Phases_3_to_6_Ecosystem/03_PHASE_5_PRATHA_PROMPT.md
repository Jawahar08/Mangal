# PHASE 5 — PRATHA PROMPT

Read the Master Prompt, feature index, repository audit, and prior-phase product architecture. Extend MangalSutra with Pratha as an optional spirituality and wellbeing layer that supports relationship nurturing while respecting diverse beliefs.

## Experiences

Offer user-selected journeys such as:

- Traditional: temple visits, pooja, rituals.
- Wellness: meditation, yoga, and appropriate wellbeing practices.
- Modern Spiritual: mindfulness and relationship practices.
- Family: festivals and family rituals.
- Couple: joint meditation, gratitude, and prayer.

Potential services include daily spiritual practice, relationship rituals, temple experiences, pooja, meditation, yoga, spiritual learning, festival calendar, couple prayers, home rituals, and personalized recommendations.

## UX and boundaries

Participation is optional. Ask about preferences rather than assuming religion, gender roles, or family practice. Let users choose, change, or clear preferences. Make it clear who can see personal or couple participation. Shared couple practices require mutual agreement. Do not present spiritual content as clinical care or as a substitute for qualified health support.

## Architecture

Inspect existing identity, navigation, service, and vendor patterns. If Pratha is a separate platform, describe service boundaries and a future single-sign-on option without adding unnecessary migrations now. Use real providers/services only when confirmed; label unavailable experiences as future plans rather than active booking.

## Acceptance criteria

A user can browse suitable offerings, understand their nature and provider, choose personal or mutually agreed couple content, and leave the experience without affecting matchmaking. Recommendations are preference-driven, private, accessible, and respectful. Report actual integrations and blockers.
